## Source and handling principle

This ledger records information extracted from the Travel Corner promotional materials supplied by the user on 17 August 2026. The material is used as **business reference data**, not as website artwork or a website layout reference. Where the poster clearly communicates a field, it may be surfaced as editable web content. Where a hotel name, location, date, or condition cannot be read with sufficient confidence from the supplied artwork, it is deliberately left out of the initial published data rather than guessed.

## Publishing safeguards

The visual website must show the six buyer-critical facts — **hotel, location, dates, meal plan, transport, and price** — close together. It must also visually distinguish campaign-reference rates from currently approved active offers, and show availability only when entered by staff. WhatsApp copy must use a context-aware English or Arabic message based on the offer or Umrah program being viewed.
